Hi! I'm a composer, laptop improvisor, and media artist based in Irvine, California. I make avant garde music and collaborate with musicians, visual artists, poets, filmmakers, and researchers.

Broadly speaking, I am interested in the meanings of creativity in the context of technology. I currently focus on laptop aesthetics, musical instrument design, and music's intersection with capital.
