Hi! I'm a composer, laptop improviser, and media artist based in Irvine, California. I make experimental music and collaborate with musicians, visual artists, poets, filmmakers, and researchers.

I am interested in the meanings of creativity in the context of technology. In my PhD at UC Irvine, I focus on laptop aesthetics, instrument design, and music's entanglement with capital.
