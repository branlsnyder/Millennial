I composed [an audio essay](http://www.estuaries808.com/blog/leadingme-e9x42?fbclid=IwAR0uKaX29yWhFtUoU01LzYKbGqnBPO7Fv83MH9qts_uqfSvcvYy0cD3WOhM) reflecting and responding to my 10 years as a worship leader (2008-2018). It is also my online-debut as a singer. While all my music and writing is personal, this one is comes from a particular place of vulnerability. It addresses myself in a way much more explicit than in my more abstract pieces.

The piece is a part of [BETWEEN LANDS](http://www.estuaries808.com/between-lands), a multimedia publication run by ESTUARIES. It is a collection of writing, music, research, and art documenting a collaborative experiment of creating a platform for building culture.
