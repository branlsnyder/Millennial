Browser Sound is a festival and community focused on music in/on/around/through the web. I started this festival in 2020 and ran it until 2024. We commissioned works of art, held workshops, and gathered artists and coders together. The festival has its [own website](https://browsersound.com/), where you can view the commisioned artists, their work, and the events we ran.
