## Program Note

A creative exercise in overthinking, "Music about Music about Background Music" stuffs 'music' and 'about music' into a singular 'music about music,' where all commentary is maxmimally musicalized, and all music is composed as commentary on the music(alized commentary) that comments on the music. 

The second iteration of a Music About Music About Background Music project
