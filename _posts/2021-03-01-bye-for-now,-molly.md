In 2021 the SWR Vokalensemble gave me a generous reading of my piece "Bye For Now, Molly", in which the singers perform transcriptions of sobs, howls, and convulsing aches, and other actions of an "ugly cry". 

text by: some heartbroken soul on reddit

Recorded rehearsal by the SWR Vokalensemble. Part of the [SWR VE Akademie für Komposition](https://www.swr.de/swrkultur/musik-klassik/vokalensemble/bildergalerie-akademisten-20-21-100.html)
