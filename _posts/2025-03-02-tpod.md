Duo with poet Lydia T Liu 

"The Problem(s) of Deer" is an improvisational duo project combining live poetry and electronic music by Lydia Liu and Brandon Woo Snyder. Approaching speech as both a semantic and a melodic material, Lydia and I react to each other in real time to create uniquely moving experiences of intersecting sound and language.
