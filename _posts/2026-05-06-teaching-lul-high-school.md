Mentored 4-8 high school-age composers through the process of composing a new work for a chamber ensemble of their choice, with or without electronics.
- Provided weekly, 1-hour individual private lessons
- Taught audio recording and microphone fundamentals using Reaper, for the purpose of electroacoustic composition.
- Taught composition fundamentals, as needed for each composer-student, including harmony, voice leading, score-notation, and post-tonal methods.
