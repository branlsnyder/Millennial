## Program Note

tree and synthetic. (breath - stasis ahead one final and forward) is

 

an environment to take in,

 

                             rich with terrain, objects, organisms, and atmosphere.

 

 

 

 

 

It is one to be perpetually remembering and forgetting

                      

                          the things which are among us always.
