Self-built Midi Piano Instrument

In 2025 I was invited as an alumni fellow to perform a short solo set at the Line Upon Line Winter Composers Festival. The performance revolves around a self-built midi piano instrument, where I control thousands of piano keys per minute. At times the music feels like large rain clouds of hail, other times like soft mist. At even other times the music breaks in and out of rhythmic and harmonic grids, similar to how a magnetic ball bearing might snap on and off a track.
